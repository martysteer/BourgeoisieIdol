# Bourgeoisie

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourgeoisie

**Theme:** Class systems, social dynamics

## Key Concepts

The bourgeoisie is a social class characterized by ownership of capital and means of production. The term evolved from medieval French to describe the merchant and artisan classes, eventually becoming central to Marxist analysis of capitalist society.

## Relevance to BourgeoisieIdol

- Class stratification and social hierarchy
- Power dynamics between different participant roles
- Ownership and control of cultural/creative production
- Economic relationships embedded in creative competition
