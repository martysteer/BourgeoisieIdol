# Popular Assembly

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_assembly

**Theme:** Collective decision-making

## Key Concepts

Popular assemblies are gatherings where community members participate directly in decision-making processes. Found throughout history from ancient Athens to modern movements, they represent direct democracy in action.

## Relevance to BourgeoisieIdol

- Direct participation versus representative structures
- Collective judgment and decision-making
- Democratic processes in cultural evaluation
- Community governance of shared experiences
