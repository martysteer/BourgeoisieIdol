# Soviet Mosaics (1980s)

**Source:** https://www.reddit.com/r/RetroFuturism/comments/j0904z/soviet_mosaics_a_lot_of_them_made_in_the_80s/

**Theme:** Visual aesthetic

## Key Concepts

Soviet mosaics from the 1980s represent a distinct visual language combining socialist realism, geometric abstraction, and public art. Often featuring bold colors, angular forms, and themes of collective labor and progress.

## Relevance to BourgeoisieIdol

- Bold, geometric visual design language
- Public art and collective experience
- Retro-futurist aesthetics
- Socialist visual culture and symbolism
- Integration of art into social spaces
