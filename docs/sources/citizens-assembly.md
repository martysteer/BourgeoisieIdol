# Citizens' Assembly

**Source:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizens%27_assembly

**Theme:** Direct democracy

## Key Concepts

Citizens' assemblies are deliberative bodies composed of randomly or demographically selected citizens who study and make recommendations on specific issues. They combine elements of direct democracy with informed deliberation.

## Relevance to BourgeoisieIdol

- Structured deliberation processes
- Random selection versus elite selection
- Informed decision-making frameworks
- Balancing expertise with democratic participation
