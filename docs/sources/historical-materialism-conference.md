# Twenty-third Annual Historical Materialism Conference

**Event:** Planetary Warscapes Ablaze: Imperial Mutations in Infernal Times

**Source:** https://www.historicalmaterialism.org/event/twenty-third-annual-conference/

## Streams

### Marxism, Culture and the Arts Stream
**URL:** https://www.historicalmaterialism.org/event/twenty-third-annual-conference/#marxismculture

Focus on cultural production, artistic practice, and aesthetic theory from Marxist perspectives.

### New Technologies and Capital Accumulation
**URL:** https://www.historicalmaterialism.org/event/twenty-third-annual-conference/#newtechnologies

Examines how emerging technologies relate to capitalist accumulation, labor transformation, and social organization.

## Relevance to BourgeoisieIdol

BourgeoisieIdol is being developed for presentation at this conference, engaging with:
- Cultural production under capitalism
- Technology and creative labor
- Alternative models of artistic evaluation
- Materialist analysis of game show formats and competitive culture
