# Twenty-third Annual Historical Materialism Conference 2026

**Event:** Planetary Warscapes Ablaze: Imperial Mutations in Infernal Times

**Source:** https://www.historicalmaterialism.org/event/twenty-third-annual-conference/

**Date:** November 5-8, 2026

**Location:** SOAS, Russell Square, Central London

**Abstract Deadline:** Friday, May 16, 2025

## About Historical Materialism

The journal **Historical Materialism: Research in Critical Marxist Theory** organises the London conference, which is the **largest academic Marxist conference in the world**.

## Conference Theme

**Planetary Warscapes Ablaze: Imperial Mutations in Infernal Times** addresses contemporary crises of imperialism, climate catastrophe, and capitalist transformation in an era of intensifying global conflict.

## Marxism, Culture and the Arts Stream

**URL:** https://www.historicalmaterialism.org/event/twenty-third-annual-conference/#marxismculture

The conference invites collective consideration of concepts of **cultural and artistic resistance, obstinacy, struggle, subversion and inertia**, with particular attention to how these have opened up local, national and international networks of proletarian, antifascist and anti-imperialist practitioners.

### Key Questions

- How have notions of resistance shaped the history of cultural and artistic form?
- How have colonised peoples subverted the attempt at installing cultural hegemony?
- How has proletarian cultural education suffered and confronted recent offences?

## New Technologies and Capital Accumulation Stream

**URL:** https://www.historicalmaterialism.org/event/twenty-third-annual-conference/#newtechnologies

Examines how emerging technologies relate to capitalist accumulation, labor transformation, and social organization.

## Other Specialized Streams

The conference features various specialized streams, including a dedicated stream on **Marxism & Disability** at the 2026 Historical Materialism London conference.
